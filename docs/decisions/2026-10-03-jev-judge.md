# Jev decides what scout7 keeps and draws, called directly

- **Problem**: gemma4's extraction found an "architecture" in 29 of 30 pages (rankings, funding news included) and asked for 8 diagrams where 2 were warranted.
- **Decision**: a System One judge (Jev on Cloudflare) answers typed questions before extraction: keep if `describes_architecture` >= 0.5 and `product_listing` < 0.5, draw if `novelty` >= 1.0 (0-3 scale); gemma4 keeps the extraction. A failed judge call falls back to the LLM.
- **Why direct, not through the mesh**: the mesh governs agents; Jev is a model, like the LLM endpoint sup7 already calls directly. If models need control later, a gateway goes in front of it through `judge.url`.
- **Where**: `agent/judge.go`, `agent/loop.go`, `config.go` (`judge:`), `scout7.yaml`; bench in `~/work/jev/banc-scout7` (30 pages, 2 positives: thresholds to revisit on a larger bench).
